Font used by this site

Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, by the Braille Institute of America, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (Atkinson_OFL.txt in this folder).
Source: github.com/google/fonts (ofl/atkinsonhyperlegiblenext).
Weights 300, 400 and 650. The files are reduced copies that keep Latin letters, punctuation, arrows and mathematical signs.
